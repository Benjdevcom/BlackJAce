Gameplay

  • The Goal: Hit or stand just like in standard Blackjack, but watch out for your opponent's moves.

  • The AI Opponent: Your enemies are driven by an active AI that reads the current state of the table, plays its own modifiers, and tries to counter your moves.

  • Break the Rules: Standard cards will only get you so far. During your run, you will collect and play Special Cards (such as score modifiers, multipliers, reverses, and negates).

  • Synergies: Combine special cards creatively. Use a Negate to block an opponent's dangerous play or stack multipliers to turn a losing hand into a massive win.

  • The Journey: Fight your way through 4 hazardous zones: Cave, Forest, Ice, and Lava. Each environment features custom atmospheric details tailored to your opponent.

  • Secret Rooms: Keep your eyes open during your descent. You can stumble upon hidden rooms containing rare, powerful cards that can alter your entire strategy.

  • Consumables vs. Permanent Upgrades: Visit the shop to buy immediate consumables (like health potions and energy scrolls) or invest in game-changing permanent upgrades (such as unlocking more special card slots or increasing your starting health) that persist from one run to the next.

  • Boss Fights: Reach the end of a zone and face a major boss. Defeating a boss finishes your current run.
